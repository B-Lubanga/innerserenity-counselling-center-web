Technical Tips

To ensure the best experience for your virtual session, please:

  • Test your camera and microphone beforehand
  • Find a quiet, private space where you won't be interrupted
  • Ensure you have a stable internet connection
  • Use Chrome or Firefox browsers for best results
  • Close unnecessary tabs and applications to improve performance

What to Prepare

To make the most of your session, we recommend having:

  • A notepad and pen for taking notes
  • Any previous session notes or relevant documents
  • A glass of water
  • Headphones for better audio quality (optional)

Cancellation Policy
